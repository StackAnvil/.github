# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect in issues, reviews, and community conversations.
Give specific feedback about the work. Listen to corrections and assume different levels of experience.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, spam, and disclosure of another person's private information are unacceptable.
Maintainers can remove disruptive content or restrict participation to protect the community.

For a concern, contact the maintainers through the channels in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md).
Ask for a private channel before sharing sensitive details. Do not publish another person's private information in a report.
